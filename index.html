How we pick

Every prediction here goes through the same filter: recent form, head-to-head history, and squad news — no gut feelings, no "vibes" picks. If a match doesn't check enough boxes, it doesn't get posted, even if that means a quiet day. We'd rather post fewer tips and keep a record worth trusting than flood the page for the sake of it.
